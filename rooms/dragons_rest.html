Dragon's Rest

Dragon's Rest is our warmest and most inviting guest room. Inspired by the hidden chambers of an ancient mountain keep, the room combines rich wood furnishings, warm lighting, and fantasy-inspired decorations with modern comfort.

After a long day of exploring, guests can relax beside the fireplace, enjoy a peaceful night in the queen-size bed, and wake to a complimentary homemade breakfast.

Room Amenities

  • Queen-size bed
  • Private bathroom
  • Electric fireplace
  • Free Wi-Fi
  • Complimentary breakfast
  • Coffee and tea station
