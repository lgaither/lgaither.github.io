Feywood Suite

Step into an enchanted woodland retreat in the Feywood Suite. Inspired by ancient forests and stories of the Feywild, this spacious room features natural wood furnishings, greenery, and soft lighting designed to create a peaceful atmosphere.

The Feywood Suite is ideal for guests looking for a quiet and relaxing getaway. Large windows overlook the inn's gardens, while the comfortable king-size bed provides the perfect place to rest after a day of adventure.
