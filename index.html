Welcome to The Moonlit Griffin

Hidden among ancient trees and quiet countryside, The Moonlit Griffin Bed & Breakfast offers travelers a peaceful escape from the ordinary. Inspired by the warmth and mystery of classic fantasy inns, our bed and breakfast combines comfortable accommodations with the atmosphere of a storybook adventure.

A Stay Worth Remembering

Guests of The Moonlit Griffin can enjoy uniquely decorated rooms, homemade breakfast each morning, relaxing outdoor spaces, and a quiet setting perfect for unwinding after a long journey.

  • Comfortable fantasy-themed guest rooms
  • Complimentary homemade breakfast
  • Outdoor gardens and firepit
  • Free Wi-Fi
  • Nearby hiking and local attractions

Your Adventure Begins Here

Whether you are searching for a relaxing weekend, a romantic getaway, or simply somewhere a little different from an ordinary hotel, The Moonlit Griffin welcomes every traveler who passes through its doors.
